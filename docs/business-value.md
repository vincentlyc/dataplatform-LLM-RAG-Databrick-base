# Business Value

## The Problem
Enterprise knowledge is often scattered across Word files, documents, standards, internal records, historical project materials, and operational documentation.

Common pain points:
- hard to search
- knowledge depends on specific individuals
- onboarding is slow
- historical decisions are difficult to trace
- repeated internal communication consumes time

## The Solution
This project transforms internal documents into a governed enterprise knowledge platform built on Databricks.

It provides:
- document ingestion
- semantic chunking
- vector retrieval
- grounded LLM answering
- source traceability
- token and cost visibility

## Value for Engineering Leadership
- makes historical knowledge easier to retrieve
- reduces repeated explanation overhead
- enables enterprise AI use cases on top of existing data platform capabilities
- demonstrates scalable architecture rather than one-off chatbot scripting

## Value for HR / Hiring Evaluation
This project demonstrates:
- enterprise platform thinking
- data engineering and AI integration
- strong solution packaging ability
- cross-functional communication capability
- cost and governance awareness

## Value for Business Stakeholders
- reduce time spent finding internal answers
- accelerate onboarding and knowledge transfer
- lower dependency on senior staff memory
- turn documents into reusable internal assets

## Industry Relevance
### Semiconductor
Applicable to SOPs, process standards, defect analysis documents, engineering records, and operational knowledge.

### Financial Services
Applicable to governance documents, payment procedures, risk controls, regulatory interpretation, internal standards, and incident knowledge.

## Strategic Significance
This project shows how a data platform can evolve into an enterprise AI knowledge platform.
That is much more valuable than a simple document chatbot.
